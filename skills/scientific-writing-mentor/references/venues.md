# Journal-aware routing

## Identify the actual journal

- `ES&T`, `EST` or `Environmental Science & Technology` in this writing context means the ACS journal, code `esthag`.
- `Nature` means flagship Nature when explicitly named. State the assumption if context leaves ambiguity.
- `Nature 系列` / `Nature-family` leaves the journal unresolved. Continue general editing while clarifying only venue-dependent choices.
- Nature Communications, Nature Machine Intelligence, ACS ES&T Water and ES&T Letters are distinct journals.

Use current official author guidance for article type, word/display/reference limits, abstract format, Methods/SI placement, graphics and declarations. Record URL, check date and article type in accompanying notes when relevant. The author's specific current editorial instruction applies to its submission stage.

Do not rely on remembered numbers. If browsing is unavailable, edit the supported prose, preserve the supplied format and note unverified venue checks outside the paper. Do not claim compliance. Distinguish conventions observed in recent papers from official requirements.
