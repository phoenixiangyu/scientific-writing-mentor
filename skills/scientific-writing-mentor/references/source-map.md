## S1 — supplied teaching presentation

邵森林, *写好科技论文的一些建议*, presentation dated 2021-11-04, 24 slides. The public teaching copy has contact information removed; attribution remains.

| Slide | Teaching theme | Operational adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| 4–5 | Scientific paper versus report; engineering case versus scientific question | Organize around what the study establishes, not experiment chronology. |
| 7–9 | Early outline, motivation, hypothesis, key tests and feedback from results | Co-develop a provisional outline and figure sequence, then update it. |
| 11–12 | Titles and selective quantitative abstracts | Name the contribution and use the numbers needed for the message. |
| 14–15 | Introduction, relevant literature, question and scope | Guide the reader from known context to the specific purpose. |
| 16–17 | Coherence, compact paragraphs and conclusion | Keep one useful paragraph function and a consistent main line. Treat suggested paragraph lengths as heuristics. |
| 18–19 | Readable related figures and captions | Align text, displays and legends. Typography follows current venue rules. |

| 23 | Tense overview | Choose tense by function and check the source meaning. |

## Author-defined additions

The requesting author specified fluent clean prose with evidence questions in anchored comments; cooperation with installed skills such as Nature Skills; ES&T/Nature routing; automatic selection where supported; and operation without mandatory collaborators. These requirements are implemented in the entrypoint and comments, venues and collaboration references.

Version 2 adds the author's advisor priorities: focused natural prose, concise sentences, short sufficient captions, purposeful Results subsection openings, explicit paragraph conclusions and useful closing inferences, transitions, value-led presentation with necessary boundaries and related-figure synthesis. Version 2.0.1 removes the withdrawn Methods/non-Methods structure rule: structural intervention follows the task and explicit user constraints. These are author preferences and corrections, not claims about universal journal rules; see [advisor-priorities.md](advisor-priorities.md).
