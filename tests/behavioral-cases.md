# Behavioral review cases

These are concrete acceptance scenarios, not proof that every scenario has been independently tested. Version 1.0's review was a local walkthrough and helper tests. Version 2.0 adds a bounded independent Results/caption revision check; see [the recorded outcome](v2-behavior-check.md). The full multi-paper literature workflow remains untested end to end.

1. **“按照 ES&T 润色这段结果” with only measured means.** Edit the paragraph; keep values and baselines; place uncertainty requests in comments; do not use Nature's summary format or manufacture significance.
2. **“按照 Nature 系列改摘要” without a journal.** Continue audience/argument edits, state that the exact journal is unresolved, and ask only before applying journal-specific format.
3. **“写论文大纲” with planned experiments.** Separate observations from plans; organize by the scientific question; do not write predicted findings as completed results.
4. **“去掉防御性写作” with a real failure at high salinity.** Remove empty self-defence but retain the failure condition and limit the claim accordingly, even if anti-defensive-writing is installed.
5. **A mechanism sentence with no discriminating evidence.** Use an association-level supported formulation or withhold the unsupported clause; anchor the question in the sidecar. No invented citation.
6. **Only one sentence supplied and no collaborators installed.** Perform the bounded edit from bundled rules; do not require a full manuscript, API setup or installation.
7. **DOCX output requested, native comments unavailable.** Produce a revision plus anchored sidecar and explicitly describe the fallback; do not claim native comments were written.
8. **An author asks to finalize with unresolved blocking issues.** Report the unresolved items outside the manuscript; do not call it submission-ready.
9. **Related performance and resource-use panels, followed by a salinity failure.** Orient the subsection before numbers, integrate the related panels where useful, give paragraphs explicit supported conclusions, and connect the failure condition to the scope without hiding it or claiming a mechanism.
10. **A verbose caption lacks n and an error-bar definition.** Remove repeated discussion, retain supplied conditions and panel identities, and put requests for missing statistics in comments rather than inventing them.
11. **Editing scope across sections.** For language-only work or an explicitly fixed outline, preserve organization in every section. For a requested Results logic revision, reorganize related paragraphs where useful; for requested Methods language polish, do not infer permission to reconstruct its organization. Explain consequential moves and retain all research facts.
12. **A very short Results paragraph already closes with a clear inference.** Keep the conclusion without appending another generic significance sentence. Sentence compression must retain comparison groups, conditions and units.
