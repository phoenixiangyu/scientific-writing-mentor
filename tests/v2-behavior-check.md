# v2.0 bounded independent writing check

One independent agent was given the new skill path and a synthetic Results subsection/caption, with no proposed revision or expected answer. It read relevant guidance and returned a revision without browsing, editing the repository, or using other writing skills. This is one observed case, not a statistical assessment of writing quality or automatic trigger reliability.

## Supplied material

The fixed heading was `3.2 Treatment performance`. Three paragraphs separately reported Fig. 2a nitrate removal at 25 °C (modified/reference: 82%/70%), Fig. 2b electricity use at 25 °C (0.06/0.09 kWh/m³), and Fig. 3 removal at 20 g/L NaCl (40%/65%). The source repeated its own results and included generic praise and future-work language. The verbose caption supplied panel identities and 25 °C but no n, error bars or tests. Only those values and conditions were confirmed.

## Observed revision

**3.2 Treatment performance**

Nitrate removal and electricity use were compared to assess the treatment performance of the modified and reference reactors. At 25 °C, nitrate removal was higher in the modified reactor than in the reference reactor (82% versus 70%; Fig. 2a), while electricity use was lower (0.06 versus 0.09 kWh/m³; Fig. 2b). Thus, higher nitrate removal coincided with lower electricity use under this condition.

At 20 g/L NaCl, however, nitrate removal was lower in the modified reactor than in the reference reactor (40% versus 65%; Fig. 3), indicating a performance disadvantage for the modified reactor under this high-salinity condition.

**Figure 2.** Nitrate removal (a) and electricity use (b) in the modified and reference reactors operated at 25 °C.

The agent separately asked for statistical definitions if the actual display contains error bars or statistical marks, and noted that the temperature of the high-salinity test had not been supplied. These notes did not enter the clean manuscript prose.

## Review of this outcome

The heading and evidence order stayed intact. The related panels were integrated into one paragraph, the subsection opened with the comparison purpose, and both resulting paragraphs closed with an explicit bounded inference. All supplied values, conditions and figure references remained; the unfavorable high-salinity finding was retained. The caption kept the supplied reading information without generic praise. No significance, mechanism, replication or high-salinity temperature was invented.

This case did not exercise Methods restructuring, a complete manuscript, document rendering, host selection without an explicit skill path, other-skill collaboration or the three-paper literature workflow. Those capabilities are not claimed as validated by this check.
